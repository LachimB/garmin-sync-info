A private, single-user Android utility that collects Garmin Connect activities, health, weight and fitness data, prepares local exports and publishes them to the owner's Google Drive.

01 / Collect

Activities, health and fitness

Collect activities, sleep, heart-rate variability, wellness sessions, heart rate, SpO2, stress, supported Body Battery aggregates, weight and fitness observations. Current weight goals and heart-rate zones retain observed changes going forward. Source availability and resolution limits stay explicit.

02 / Export

Files you can use

Activity and daily health JSON, monthly weight and fitness files, dated goal and zone histories, activity archives and unified sync history. Exact downloaded originals are preserved inside authenticated encryption; decrypted originals can be explicitly saved to a private destination.

Weekly archives can include or omit GPS routes. Manual controls support collection, archive creation and publishing queued files.

03 / Stay in control

Sync on your terms

Configure independent collection and backup schedules, publication windows, and Wi-Fi-only or Wi-Fi-and-mobile-data access. Historical backfill can continue across days with Pause, Resume and Cancel, shared request pacing and no app-imposed daily cap or time-of-day window. When an earliest source date is unknown, select a bounded search in the app. Scheduled sync takes priority during collection and Drive publishing. Backfill saves progress, yields when scheduled work is due and resumes automatically; an explicit Pause stays saved until Resume. Collected data and verified delivery have separate status.

Configure backfill safeguards for undelivered file count, queued size and available phone storage. Historical collection waits while eligible uploads drain and resumes automatically when safe. Automatic cleanup removes obsolete temporary files and unreferenced file versions while protecting required local records and recovery data.

Review every task run, retained retry attempts, delivered files and errors in one filterable history. Older PDF histories are imported with clear legacy labels and their originals are preserved.

Optional automatic history pruning silently removes old sync records after a configurable number of days (100 by default). It starts off; exported Garmin data, original legacy PDFs and separately saved backups are kept.

Android background scheduling is best effort. Device restrictions, connectivity and revoked access can delay work.

Limited Google Drive access

Google authorization uses drive.file for files created or explicitly opened for the app. The app checks the authorized account and saved destination before publishing.

Exports stay in the owner's Drive. This website does not host private records or offer a public app download.

Private backup and recovery

Save and verify passphrase-encrypted local app-recovery snapshots, manually or on a daily schedule. Automatic Drive copies keep the newest 10 verified automatic backups by default; manual copies are protected. Snapshots include the Garmin key, stored backup passphrase and all originals keys, but exclude Google grants and previous backup files. They do not retrieve exports stored only on Drive. Recovery requires an empty installation, new Google authorization and explicit access verification before saved work resumes.

Understand what leaves your phone

The app filters identifying fields from exported JSON, but health measurements, activity names, timestamps and GPS routes can still be sensitive. Keep exports and recovery backups private.
