Android · Personal use

Your Garmin data.
Your own exports.

A private, single-user Android utility that collects Garmin Connect activities and wellness data, prepares local exports and publishes them to the owner's Google Drive.

01 / Collect

Activities and daily wellness

Collect recent activities, retrieve older activities and refresh daily wellness records, including sleep, heart-rate variability, Body Battery and stress. Optional GPS routes can be included in activity archives.

02 / Export

Files you can use

Individual activity JSON files, a configurable latest-activity index, daily health JSON files, full or date-range activity archives, and a unified sync history.

Weekly archives can include or omit GPS routes. Manual controls support collection, archive creation and publishing queued files.

03 / Stay in control

Sync on your terms

Configure schedules and choose Wi-Fi only or Wi-Fi and mobile data. Pause Drive publishing, inspect pending work and failed checks, and repair access when needed.

Review every task run, retained retry attempts, delivered files and errors in one filterable history. Older PDF histories are imported with clear legacy labels and their originals are preserved.

Optional automatic history pruning silently removes old sync records after a configurable number of days (100 by default). It starts off; exported Garmin data, original legacy PDFs and separately saved backups are kept.

Android background scheduling is best effort. Device restrictions, connectivity and revoked access can delay work.

Limited Google Drive access

Google authorization uses drive.file for files created or explicitly opened for the app. The app checks the authorized account and saved destination before publishing.

Exports stay in the owner's Drive. This website does not host private records or offer a public app download.

Private backup and recovery

Save and verify passphrase-encrypted recovery backups for an empty replacement installation. Credentials are provisioned separately, and restored publishing remains paused until the saved account and destination are verified.

Understand what leaves your phone

The app filters identifying fields from exported JSON, but health measurements, activity names, timestamps and GPS routes can still be sensitive. Keep exports and recovery backups private.
