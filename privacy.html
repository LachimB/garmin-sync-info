Privacy Policy

Garmin Sync is a personal, single-user Android utility. Data is processed on the owner's phone and exported to the owner's Google Drive.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Data accessed

The app retrieves Garmin Connect activity and wellness information authorized by its owner. Depending on the task and settings, this includes activity names, types, dates, performance metrics, workout details, GPS routes, sleep, heart-rate metrics, heart-rate variability, Body Battery and stress.

Google Drive access uses the drive.file scope, limited to files created or explicitly opened for this app. The app reads the signed-in account's email address and authorized file and folder metadata to verify the saved owner, destination and access. It does not request general access to all Drive content.

Purpose and storage

The app stores records, caches, settings, pending exports and run history in app-private phone storage. It prepares activity and daily health JSON exports, activity archives and PDF publication histories, then uploads them to the saved Google Drive destination for the owner's use.

Scheduled and manual sync follow the selected Wi-Fi-only or Wi-Fi-and-mobile-data preference. Pausing publishing stops Drive publication; it does not delete local records or existing Drive files.

The app filters identifying JSON fields before export. This does not make the remaining data anonymous: activity names, dates, health metrics and GPS tracks may reveal personal details or sensitive locations.

Credentials and recovery

The Garmin API key and filename key use Android Keystore-backed encryption on the phone. Google access is authorized through Google services. Credentials are excluded from exported records and recovery backups.

App-private data is excluded from ordinary Android system backups. The owner can explicitly save and verify a passphrase-encrypted recovery backup at a chosen location. It contains private records, routes, settings and recovery state, so both the file and its passphrase require protection and should be kept separately.

Recovery requires an empty installation. Credentials are provisioned separately; publishing remains paused until the saved account and destination are verified.

Sharing and third parties

The utility communicates with Garmin to retrieve authorized data and with Google to authorize access and store exports in Drive. Those providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The utility does not sell Garmin data or publish it on this website. Any additional sharing of Drive files or recovery backups is controlled separately by the owner and the chosen services. Drive permissions and copies shared elsewhere must be managed separately.

Retention, access and deletion

Local data and uploaded exports remain until removed by the owner. Google access can be revoked in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete files already uploaded.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data and can destroy pending work; it does not remove Drive exports or separately saved recovery backups. To remove those copies, delete them from their respective storage locations and manage any retained trash or shared copies there.

This website

These static GitHub Pages are informational only. They provide no login, forms, analytics scripts or tracking cookies, and do not collect Garmin or Google credentials or host private records. GitHub may process standard hosting and security logs under its own policies.

This policy is updated when the app's data handling changes. The current version remains available at this URL.
