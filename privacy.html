Privacy Policy

Garmin Sync is a personal, single-user Android utility. Data is processed on the owner's phone and exported to the owner's Google Drive.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Data accessed

The app retrieves Garmin Connect activity and wellness information authorized by its owner. Depending on the task and settings, this includes activity names, types, dates, performance metrics, workout details, GPS routes, sleep, heart-rate metrics, heart-rate variability, supported Body Battery aggregates, stress, SpO2, wellness sessions, weight/body composition, fitness observations, current weight goals and heart-rate zones. Source dates, availability and aggregate resolution are retained without promising every native sensor reading. Ordinary collection excludes food/hydration logs, user profile settings, activity catalogs and women’s health.

Google Drive access uses the drive.file scope, limited to files created or explicitly opened for this app. The app reads the signed-in account's email address and authorized file and folder metadata to verify the saved owner, destination and access. It does not request general access to all Drive content.

Purpose and storage

The app stores records, caches, settings, pending exports and run history in app-private phone storage. It prepares activity and daily health JSON, monthly weight/fitness files, dated goal/zone changes, activity archives and structured sync history, then uploads them to the saved Google Drive destination for the owner's use.

New sync history records include task times, outcomes, sanitized error descriptions and references to exported files. History remains in app-private storage, is copied to the private Drive destination and can be saved as a filtered CSV. Older PDF histories are imported with their original text and source references; the original PDFs remain preserved. History and exported copies may reveal personal activity dates and should be kept private.

Scheduled and manual sync follow the selected Wi-Fi-only or Wi-Fi-and-mobile-data preference. Pausing publishing stops Drive publication; it does not delete local records or existing Drive files.

The app filters identifying JSON fields before export. This does not make the remaining data anonymous: activity names, dates, health metrics and GPS tracks may reveal personal details or sensitive locations. Exact downloaded originals retain all returned content, including identifiers and GPS, inside authenticated encryption. Explicit decrypted export produces sensitive plaintext that must be stored privately.

Credentials and recovery

The Garmin API key, filename key, versioned originals keys and stored backup passphrase use Android Keystore-backed protection on the phone. Credentials are excluded from ordinary readable exports. Passphrase-encrypted recovery snapshots include the Garmin API key, filename key, every originals key and stored backup passphrase so a replacement installation can recover them. Google access tokens, grants and device Keystore private keys are excluded and Google access must be authorized again.

App-private data is excluded from ordinary Android system backups. The owner can save and verify a passphrase-encrypted recovery backup privately or request a manual Drive copy. Configurable daily automatic Drive backups default to Wi-Fi only. Snapshots include persistent local records, originals, settings, pending work and recovery secrets. They exclude previous backup files and do not download ordinary exports stored only on Drive. They are local app recovery, not an independent copy of all Drive data. Keep the file private and retain its passphrase separately.

Recovery requires an empty installation. Restored execution remains held until the owner confirms any other installation is stopped and verifies the saved account, identities and access. Recurring Garmin collection stays off, and an explicit user backfill pause is preserved. Older backups may require separate Garmin-key entry.

Sharing and third parties

The utility communicates with Garmin to retrieve authorized data and with Google to authorize access and store exports in Drive. Those providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The utility does not sell Garmin data or publish it on this website. Any additional sharing of Drive files or recovery backups is controlled separately by the owner and the chosen services. Drive permissions and copies shared elsewhere must be managed separately.

Retention, access and deletion

Automatic local cleanup removes expired request caches, obsolete temporary and staging files, and file versions no longer referenced by retained data. Required records, originals, keys, unfinished uploads, reusable archives and protected local recovery copies are retained. Configurable backfill queue and free-storage limits can temporarily stop historical collection without overriding an explicit user Pause.

Retained local records and uploaded Garmin exports remain until removed by the owner. Optional sync-history pruning is off by default. When enabled, a positive retention period in days is required (100 by default); older sync records and their attempt and file details are automatically deleted from the local history without notifications. Undated imported entries age from their import time. The structured Drive history copies are updated when publishing and connectivity allow, including clearing months with no remaining records. Exported Garmin data, queued work, original legacy PDFs, previously saved CSV files and recovery backups are not deleted by this setting. Automatic recovery rotation is separate: by default it keeps the newest 10 verified automatic Drive copies, using a configurable positive count. Verification precedes deletion, and manual, external, pending or unknown copies are protected. Rotation does not delete ordinary exports, local records or decryption keys. Google access can be revoked in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete files already uploaded.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data and can destroy pending work; it does not remove Drive exports or separately saved recovery backups. To remove those copies, delete them from their respective storage locations and manage any retained trash or shared copies there.
